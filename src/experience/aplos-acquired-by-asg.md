  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Aplos was acquired by Alpine SG (ASG) for an undisclosed amount on December 21, 2021.  See the <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220223005285/en/Aplos-Joins-ASG-to-Scale-Up-Industry-leading-Nonprofit-Software-Platform">Press Release</a>.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Aplos is a developer of a web-based nonprofit management and accounting software designed to simplify managing a non-profit organization. The company's software offers web-based tools for fund accounting, online donations, tax preparation, donor management, donation processing, and Form 990 preparation, enabling non-profits and churches to track and efficiently manage the financial health of the organization.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>ASG is an owner and operator of vertical SaaS companies in the United States. The company operates as a fast growing software business that buys, builds, and operates market-leading vertical SaaS companies by incorporating exceptional talent, deep data analytics, and a market-tested software playbook to uplift a company's efficiency to the next level.</p>
