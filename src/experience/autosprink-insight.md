<p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, CA </strong>- Peak Technology Partners is pleased to announce its role as exclusive financial advisor to <strong>M.E.P.CAD, Inc.</strong>, the developer of AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD, on its sale to <strong>VisualLogix, Inc.</strong>, a portfolio company of<strong> Insight Partners</strong> and an AI-enabled software company offering best-in-class, mission-critical software solutions used by engineers and fabricators.</p><p>The transaction added AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD to VisualLogix's growing portfolio of software solutions that help customers design, analyze, document, and manufacture real-world products and systems.</p><p><strong>Best-in-Class Software for Fire Protection Design</strong></p><p>AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD are category-leading software solutions purpose-built for fire sprinkler and fire alarm system design. The products enable engineers, contractors, and designers to perform hydraulic calculations, create intelligent 2D and 3D models, coordinate system designs, and generate fabrication-ready documentation with speed and precision. Trusted by fire protection professionals throughout North America and internationally, the solutions help customers reduce error-prone manual processes and deliver accurate, code-compliant designs for projects of every scale.</p><blockquote><p>“AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD are exactly the type of mission-critical, best-in-class software solutions around which we are building VisualLogix,” said Brett Zane, Chief Executive Officer of VisualLogix. “These products have earned the trust of fire protection professionals through decades of innovation, deep domain expertise, and a strong commitment to customers. By bringing them into one unified software company alongside our other engineering and fabrication solutions, we can invest more broadly in product development, artificial intelligence, and customer success while preserving the specialized expertise that makes each solution a category leader. PEAK provided thoughtful advice and steady support throughout the transaction, and we appreciated the preparation, market insight, and hands-on guidance its team brought to the process.”</p></blockquote><p><strong>About AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD</strong></p><p>AutoSPRINK and AlarmCAD are industry-leading software solutions for the design of fire sprinkler and fire alarm systems. Their proprietary design capabilities and AutoCAD- and Revit-compatible workflows enable fire protection engineers, contractors, and designers to model, calculate, coordinate, and document systems in 2D and 3D. The products support projects ranging from single-family residential construction to large-scale commercial and industrial facilities and are trusted by customers throughout North America and internationally.</p><p><strong>About VisualLogix</strong></p><p>VisualLogix develops mission-critical CAD, simulation, analysis, fabrication, and AI-powered software used by engineers and fabricators designing real-world systems and products. The company's solutions help customers design, analyze, document, and manufacture real-world products and systems that power the modern world.</p><p>Its portfolio includes AutoSPRINK, AlarmCAD, Metal Building Software (MBS), Athena, Apollon, TrunCAD, nCAD, flixo, and Triviso, along with other specialized software solutions trusted by customers around the world. Through continuous investment in technology, innovation, and customer success, VisualLogix enables professionals to solve complex engineering and fabrication challenges with confidence. VisualLogix is a portfolio company of Insight Partners.</p><p><strong>About Peak Technology Partners</strong></p><p>Peak Technology Partners is a boutique investment bank focused on helping innovative founders and growth-stage companies achieve exceptional outcomes. With deep domain expertise in B2B software and technology-enabled businesses, PEAK delivers tailored sell-side advisory and M&amp;A execution. The firm combines operational experience with strategic insight to guide clients through high-impact transactions, always prioritizing founder alignment, market fit, and long-term value creation.</p>
