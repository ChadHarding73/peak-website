  <h4><strong>Deal Synopsis</strong></h4><p>In May 2024, CoachCare, a leading remote patient monitoring (RPM) and virtual care management company, received a $48 million strategic growth investment led by Integrity Growth Partners (“IGP”), a leading growth investment firm focused exclusively on software and technology-enabled businesses. <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/coachcare-announces-48-million-investment-led-by-integrity-growth-partners-302207055.html" target="_blank">Press Release.</a></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Seller Details</strong></h4><p>CoachCare, a New York-based remote patient monitoring and virtual care management company, moves healthcare providers beyond technology to a complete remote care solution addressing many healthcare specialties. Its comprehensive offering, including remote patient monitoring, chronic care management, principal care management and behavioral health integration drives improved patient outcomes and increased provider revenue. More than 150,000 patients and hundreds of healthcare organizations benefit from CoachCare’s solution.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Buyer Details</strong></h4><p>Based in Los Angeles, Integrity Growth Partners is a growth private equity firm focused on investing in lower middle market software and tech-enabled services businesses led by exceptional management teams. IGP targets investments in high-growth companies with established products and business models, bringing to bear a differentiated combination of investing and operating experience.</p><p></p>
