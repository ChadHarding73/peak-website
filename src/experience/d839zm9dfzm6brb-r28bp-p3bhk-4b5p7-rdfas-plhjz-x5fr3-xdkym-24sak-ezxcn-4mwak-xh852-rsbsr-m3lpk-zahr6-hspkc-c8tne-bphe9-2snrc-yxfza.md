  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>The company was acquired by ServiceChannel for an undisclosed amount in 2019.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Developer of a mobile interpretive application designed to helps users learn about historical places. The company's application triggers content through historic images, narrated audio, fully-interactive 3D maps, and music whenever a visitor passes by a specific part of a historic attraction, enabling users to gain information and knowledge from the palm of their hands.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Developer of a cloud-based platform for the procurement and management of commercial facilities repair and maintenance services. The company's marketplace provides facilities managers with a single platform to source, procure, manage and pay for repair and maintenance services from commercial contractors across their enterprise, enabling the clients to focus more on the big picture and to add value to their own organizations.</p>
