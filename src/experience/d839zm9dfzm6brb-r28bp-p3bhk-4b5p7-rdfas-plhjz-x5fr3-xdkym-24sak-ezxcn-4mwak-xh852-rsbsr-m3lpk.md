  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>BzzAgent provides a sampling and influencer marketing platform intended to use the power of human interaction to tell brand stories and build trust in a managed, scalable, and measured way. The company's platform offers brand targeting and engaging, brand stimulating, brand generating, brand reaching and influencing services, enabling brands to crowdsource creatively through engaging consumer generated engaging content, inspire consumer-to-consumer conversations, and drive measurable reach and sales uplift.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>PowerReviews provides an analysis of customer data services and is based in London, United Kingdom. The company helps retailers create customer loyalty and personalization programs by analyzing data.</p>
