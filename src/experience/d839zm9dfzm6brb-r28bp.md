  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Zealot Networks acquired Allscreen, a Y Combinator-backed video syndication platform for $85 million on August 13, 2015.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Allscreen distributes branded video content to places like Twitter, CNN, Fox, and Facebook to help build an audience for advertisers and publishers.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Zealot is a digital-first media company empowering entrepreneurs by delivering multi-revenue, distribution, and development opportunities.</p>
