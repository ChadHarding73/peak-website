<p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, May 19, 2025</strong> – Peak Technology Partners is pleased to announce its role as the exclusive financial advisor to <strong>Dazos, LLC</strong>—a Boca Raton, Florida-based customer relationship management (CRM) platform tailored for behavioral health providers—in securing a $25 million Series A financing round led by <strong>Radian Capital</strong>. This financing underscores the growing demand for specialized technology solutions designed to optimize behavioral healthcare services and will support Dazos' continued innovation and expansion. </p><p><strong>Pioneering Enhanced Solutions for Behavioral Health</strong> </p><p>Founded in 2018, Dazos provides a robust, cloud-based CRM and patient engagement system uniquely designed to meet the specific needs of behavioral health providers, including mental health clinics, addiction treatment centers, outpatient programs, and residential facilities. The platform streamlines patient intake processes, simplifies electronic health record management, automates clinical documentation, and enhances billing and revenue cycle management. </p><p>Behavioral health organizations using Dazos have reported notable operational improvements, including: </p><ul><li><p>Up to a 30% increase in patient admission conversion rates. </p></li><li><p>Approximately a 40% reduction in administrative workloads, allowing clinicians and staff to dedicate more time to patient care. </p></li><li><p>Improved billing accuracy and faster reimbursement cycles, significantly enhancing financial stability for provider organizations. </p></li></ul><p><strong>Accelerating Growth and Market Expansion</strong> </p><p>The Series A funding will support Dazos' strategic initiatives, including enhancing its technology platform, expanding its sales and customer support teams, and strengthening integrations within the behavioral health ecosystem. Serving customers across the United States, Dazos has quickly established itself as a critical operational resource, evidenced by consistently high customer satisfaction and retention rates. </p><p><strong>Executive Insight</strong> </p><p>David Farache, CEO of Dazos, stated, “This funding represents a significant milestone, empowering us to scale our platform and better serve the evolving needs of behavioral health providers nationwide. Our commitment remains steadfast in delivering solutions that enhance patient outcomes and operational efficiency. We look forward to our continued growth and the positive impact we'll make in partnership with Radian Capital.” </p>
