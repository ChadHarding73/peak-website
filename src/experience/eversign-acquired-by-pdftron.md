<div class="row"><div class="col span-12"><hr class="rule"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>On November 16, 2022, eversign was purchased by PDFTron, a leading document processing technology platform with PE backing from both Thoma Bravo and Silversmith Capital. The transaction allows PDFTron to further expand into the no-code space and advance their reach to the end-user market, helping organizations of all sizes fast-track their digital transformation and convergence goals.  <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pdftron-broadens-leading-document-technology-platform-with-acquisition-of-e-signature-innovator-eversign-301679534.html">Press Release</a>.</p><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><div class="row"><div class="col span-5"><h4>Seller Details</h4><p>eversign, based in Vienna and London, simplifies the signing of contracts and agreements by providing a modern, simple and affordable platform to automate business transactions while avoiding the need for legacy pen and paper. eversign counts more than 500,000 unique customers of all sizes that use the platform regularly.</p><p></p></div><div class="col span-2"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div><div class="col span-5"><h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Based in Vancouver, Canada and backed by Thoma Bravo and Silversmith Capital, PDFTron is a premier global provider of high-performance document processing technology serving OEM and enterprise customers across a broad spectrum of industries. PDFTron's market-leading SDK drives digital transformation and powers next generation software applications with dynamic document viewing, annotation, processing, and conversion capabilities, as well as advanced features such as document understanding, data extraction, and redaction.</p><p></p></div></div></div></div>
