  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>On September 23, 2021, EverWash raised $5 million from Flow Capital to accelerate growth in its membership subscription management platform for the car wash industry. <a target="_blank" href="https://www.flowcap.com/news/flow-announces-a-us-5-000-000-investment-in-everwash">Press Release.</a></p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Company Details</h4><p>EverWash is a provider of a car wash membership program that drives revenue for car wash owners. The company's application provides information on wash plans, membership and subscription plans and offers marketing support to car wash owners, enabling them to reach out to potential customers as well as save time and money.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Investor Details</h4><p>Flow Capital Corp. is a Toronto-based diversified alternative asset investor and advisor, specializing in providing minimally dilutive capital to emerging growth businesses.</p>
