  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>On March 2, 2022, EverWash raised $6 million from Spring Mountain Capital to expand its car wash membership network. <a href="https://www.prweb.com/releases/EverWash_Secures_6M_Series_B_from_Spring_Mountain_Capital_to_Expand_Car_Wash_Membership_Network/prweb18525177.htm">Press Release.</a></p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Company Details</h4><p>EverWash is a provider of a car wash membership program that drives revenue for car wash owners. The company's application provides information on wash plans, membership and subscription plans and offers marketing support to car wash owners, enabling them to reach out to potential customers as well as save time and money.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Investor Details</h4><p>Spring Mountain Capital is an investment management firm that focuses on alternative asset investing. </p>
