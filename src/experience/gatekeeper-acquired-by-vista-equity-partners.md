  <h4><strong>Deal Synopsis</strong></h4><p>Gatekeeper was acquired for over $100M by Vista Equity Partners on October 17, 2023. <a href="https://www.prweb.com/releases/gatekeeper-announces-strategic-growth-investment-from-vista-equity-partners-301956970.html">Press Release.</a></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Seller Details</strong></h4><p>Gatekeeper offers an end-to-end Vendor &amp; Contract Lifecycle Management (VCLM) SaaS solution for highly-regulated industries. Founded in 2012, Gatekeeper's multi-dimensional vendor and contract repository, best-in-class workflow engine, and intelligent third-party risk management system enables customers to restore visibility, take back control, and safeguard vendor compliance.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Buyer Details</strong></h4><p>Vista Equity Partners is a leading private equity firm with over $100B under management that invests exclusively in enterprise software and technology-enabled services. Vista Equity Partners is known for its commitment of partnering with founders to deliver value to customers, unlock potential of employees and accelerate market leadership.</p><p></p>
