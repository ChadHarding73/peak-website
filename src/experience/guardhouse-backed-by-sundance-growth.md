<p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, CA — April 6, 2026</strong> — Peak Technology Partners (PEAK), a premier software-focused investment bank, is pleased to announce our role as the exclusive financial advisor to Guardhouse on its strategic investment from Sundance Growth. </p><p>This transaction highlights PEAK’s commitment to partnering with founder-led companies that are modernizing complex, fragmented industries. Based in Sydney, Australia, Guardhouse has developed a comprehensive workforce management platform that serves as the end-to-end operational backbone for physical security providers globally. By consolidating critical operations into a single, unified digital interface, Guardhouse eliminates the friction of disjointed legacy systems. This investment will enable the company to scale its product capabilities and expand its footprint in international markets. </p><p>“Our goal at PEAK is to bridge the gap between visionary software founders and the right institutional capital,” said Scotty Williams, Managing Director at Peak Technology Partners. “The Guardhouse team has built a product-first solution that solves the industry’s heavy reliance on manual processes. It was a privilege to work alongside the Guardhouse team to achieve an outcome that reflects the strength of the platform they built, and we are proud to have facilitated this partnership with Sundance Growth to help accelerate their global trajectory.” </p><p>When asked to comment about their experience with PEAK, Guardhouse Co-Founders Andrew and Keith Longworth said, “[PEAK] was highly committed, responsive, and operated with integrity throughout. At critical moments, they went above and beyond. PEAK engaged with us early, years before we were ready to transact. At that stage, most advisors would have passed. PEAK did the opposite. They invested time upfront, effectively mentoring us on what would matter at exit when we were ready. They also had deep experience in software technology companies and had successfully exited with sales at the scale we were targeting. That gave us confidence they understood both where we were and where we needed to get to. They also built strong trust with both sides of the transaction. The buyer respected and trusted them, which materially helped in navigating challenges and maintaining momentum.”  </p><p><strong>About Guardhouse</strong></p><p>Guardhouse is the leading workforce management platform purpose-built for the physical security industry. The platform manages over 35,000 guards and facilitates more than 100,000 shifts weekly, integrating geofenced attendance and real-time compliance checks with major payroll systems. A multi-year ASIAL Security Industry Awards sponsor, Guardhouse helps security firms achieve operational precision at scale.
