<div class="row"><div class="col span-12"><hr class="rule"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>InSync was acquired by Qualifacts Systems and Credible Behavioral Health, via their financial sponsor Warburg Pincus, for an undisclosed amount on December 16, 2021.  See the <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/qualifacts--credible-acquires-insync-healthcare-solutions-301446909.html">Press Release</a>.</p><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><div class="row"><div class="col span-5"><h4>Seller Details</h4><p>InSync is a developer of configurable healthcare management software designed to manage the overall functions of the organizations. The company's cloud-based software features electronic medical records, practice management, medical billings, revenue cycle management, electronic health records, medical transcriptions, telemedicine, e-prescription (electronic), group therapy notes and antenatal flowsheet that helps to prepare chart, custom fields and configurable layouts from any laptop, tablet or mobile devices, enabling healthcare organizations to help practices enhance patient care, maintain compliance and increase revenue and productivity.</p></div><div class="col span-2"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div><div class="col span-5"><h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Qualifacts Systems is a provider of a SaaS-based behavioral health record platform. The company's web-based enterprise electronic health record (EHR) system is designed specifically for behavioral health and human services organizations, simplifying their complex business needs and extending their ability to serve.</p><p>Credible is a developer of electronic health record and practice management software. The company's electronic health record and practice management software help in managing areas like clinical, scheduling, billing, form management, reporting and business intelligence modules of behavioral health-related clinics, communities, and residencies, enabling behavioral healthcare providers to manage their processes through the application of technology.</p><p>Warburg Pincus is a private equity firm based in New York, New York. The firm seeks to invest in energy, financial services, healthcare, consumer, industrial and business services, real estate, technology, media, and telecommunication sector companies based in America, Europe, China, Southeast Asia, and India.</p></div></div></div></div>
