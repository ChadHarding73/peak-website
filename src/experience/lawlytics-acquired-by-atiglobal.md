  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>LawLytics was acquired by ATI Global for an undisclosed amount on November 16, 2021.  See the <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211116005384/en/">Press Release</a>.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>LawLytics is an operator of a cloud-based digital marketing platform intended to aid law firms to build, deploy and manage websites and blogs. The company's platform assists lawyers in building websites without overpaying a marketing agency or struggling with DIY website builders, enabling law firms to engage with customers and manage their business leads.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>ATI Global (Australian Technology Innovators) is a world-class provider of legal technology, software and information services. The ATI portfolio of companies is headquartered in Sydney, Australia with over 800 employees and a heritage of continued technology innovation and customer excellence over its 20-year history.</p>
