<div class="row"><div class="col" style="--span: 12; --of: 12"><div class="b b--html"><p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, CA – June 4, 2025</strong> – Peak Technology Partners, a leading investment bank focused on advising growth-stage technology and healthcare companies, is pleased to announce its role as the exclusive financial advisor to <strong>Limber Health</strong>, a provider of digital musculoskeletal (MSK) care and remote therapeutic monitoring (RTM) solutions, on its acquisition by <strong>Net Health</strong>, a provider of cloud-based software for specialty medical providers.</p><p>The acquisition enhances Net Health’s position in the MSK and physical therapy markets and adds robust capabilities in RTM. Limber Health’s digital care platform enables physical therapy, orthopedic, and primary care providers to deliver scalable, evidence-based care at home. The platform includes personalized exercise therapy, patient education, outcomes tracking, and RTM functionality.</p><p>Net Health serves more than 23,000 healthcare organizations across the continuum of care, including rehab therapy, wound care, home health and hospice, and employee health. With the integration of Limber Health, Net Health is expanding its offerings to support value-based care, increase access, and create new revenue opportunities through hybrid care experiences.</p><p>“I’m excited to welcome the Limber Health team, and clients, to Net Health,” said Ron Books, CEO of Net Health. “Our mission is and will always be to deliver high-value solutions and a trusted partnership that helps clients deliver better outcomes. With Limber Health joining the team, we can now support our clients across the full care continuum and extend Net Health’s reach to a true hospital-to-home experience. This acquisition extends the value we provide to rehab therapy clients and patients—especially between visits.”</p><p>“Joining forces with Net Health marks an exciting new chapter for us,” said Michael Gruner, President of Limber Health. “With the expanded resources and scale, we’re better positioned than ever to help our clients grow and improve health outcomes on a broader scale.”</p><p>Net Health is backed by The Carlyle Group, Level Equity, and Silversmith Capital Partners.</p><p>This transaction highlights Peak Technology Partners’ continued leadership in advising digital health and healthcare SaaS companies on strategic M&amp;A and capital raising activities.</p></div><div class="b b--horizontalrule"><hr class="rule"></div><div class="b b--html"><p><strong>About Limber Health</strong></p><p>Limber Health provides digital musculoskeletal care and remote therapeutic monitoring solutions for physical therapy, orthopedic, and primary care providers. Its platform enables evidence-based, at-home care supported by clinical research and is used by providers nationwide.</p><p><strong>About Net Health</strong></p><p>Net Health provides cloud-based software solutions for specialty medical providers across the continuum of care. Serving more than 23,000 healthcare organizations, Net Health’s solutions span markets including rehabilitation therapy, wound care, home health and hospice, and employee health. The company is backed by The Carlyle Group, Level Equity, and Silversmith Capital Partners.</p><p><strong>About Peak Technology Partners</strong></p><p>Peak Technology Partners is a boutique investment bank focused on advising technology companies on mergers and acquisitions, capital raising, and strategic initiatives. With deep sector expertise across healthcare and software, PEAK serves clients from offices in San Francisco and Denver.</p><p></p></div></div></div>
