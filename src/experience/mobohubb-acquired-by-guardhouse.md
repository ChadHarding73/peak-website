<div class="row"><div class="col" style="--span: 12; --of: 12"><div class="b b--html"><p><strong>DENVER, CO – August 24, 2026</strong> – Peak Technology Partners (“PEAK”), a leading investment bank focused on technology and software companies, is pleased to announce that it served as exclusive financial advisor to <strong>Mobohubb</strong>, a provider of mobile workforce management software, on its acquisition by<strong> Guardhouse</strong>, a workforce management platform purpose-built for the physical security industry. Guardhouse is backed by <strong>Sundance Growth</strong>, a growth equity firm focused on mission-critical B2B software companies. </p><p>The transaction builds on PEAK’s prior advisory services. In April 2026, PEAK served as exclusive financial advisor to Guardhouse on its strategic investment from Sundance Growth. That successful transaction gave PEAK a strong understanding of Guardhouse’s platform and growth strategy, positioning the firm to identify Mobohubb as a compelling acquisition candidate.</p><blockquote><p>“We understood where Jack Alpe, Julian Cartwright, and Christian Stewart saw the greatest opportunities for the business,” said Scotty Williams, Managing Director at Peak Technology Partners. “When we began working with Federico Peña and Mobohubb, it was clear that Guardhouse could provide the right platform for Mobohubb’s next stage of growth, while Mobohubb could add important capabilities and help accelerate Guardhouse’s expansion in the U.S. Our recent experience in the sector allowed us to recognize that strategic fit early and bring the parties together.” </p></blockquote><p>Following the transaction, Mobohubb’s team will join Guardhouse. Existing Mobohubb customers are expected to continue receiving their current services as the companies integrate their respective platforms over time. </p><p><strong>About Mobohubb </strong></p><p>Led by Federico Peña and based in Boynton Beach, Florida, Mobohubb provides mobile operations and compliance software for distributed and deskless workforces. Its platform helps security providers manage patrols, checkpoints, employee activity, tasks, reporting, and proof of service using mobile applications, GPS technology, QR codes, and cloud-based management tools. Mobohubb has served U.S. security operators for more than a decade. </p><p><strong>About Guardhouse </strong></p><p>Led by Jack Alpe and Julian Cartwright, Guardhouse provides cloud-based technology tailored to the operational needs of the physical security industry. Its software brings together guard scheduling, time and attendance, compliance management, payroll inputs, invoicing, and incident reporting. Guardhouse operates across Australia, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, and the United States.
