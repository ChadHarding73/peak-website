  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Chime Solutions received $30 million of growth capital from BBH Capital Partners on December 2, 2020. As a result of the transaction, the company was recapitalized. See the <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chime-solutions-closes-on-a-30-million-recapitalization-investment-with-bbh-capital-partners-301183574.html">Press Release</a>. </p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Company Details</h4><p>Chime Solutions is the provider of inbound contact center customer services to financial services, insurance, health care, and telecommunications sectors. The company offers appointment setting, billing assistance, benefits administration, sales lead generation and other services, enabling clients to get reliable, secure and flexible business process outsourcing services.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Investor Details</h4><p>BBH Capital Partners (“BBHCP”) provides highly customized capital solutions to lower middle market companies. Their flexible investment mandate gives the ability to act as a control or non-control investor and to structure their investments as a combination of equity and subordinated debt securities as needed.</p>
