<p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, CA – June 6, 2025</strong> – Peak Technology Partners is pleased to announce its role as exclusive financial advisor to <strong>PlusOne Solutions</strong>, a premier provider of contractor compliance and background screening services, on its acquisition by <strong>ServicePower</strong>, a global leader in AI-powered field service management software. </p><p>This strategic acquisition brings together complementary strengths and deepens ServicePower’s capabilities in contractor vetting and compliance, which is integral to supporting the blended workforce models increasingly adopted across industries. </p><p>By integrating PlusOne’s compliance technologies and contractor network with ServicePower’s scheduling, dispatch, and data-rich field workforce platform, the transaction creates a uniquely end-to-end solution for field workforce management and optimization. </p>

  <blockquote>
