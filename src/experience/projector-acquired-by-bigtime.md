  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>The company was acquired by BigTime Software, via its financial sponsor Vista Equity Partners, on July 12, 2022 for an undisclosed amount. The combined business will provide an operating platform with practice management functionality that helps professional services firms grow with confidence.  <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220712005543/en/BigTime-Software-Acquires-Projector-to-Become-Leading-Operating-Platform-for-Professional-Services-Firms">Press Release</a>.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Projector PSA provides professional services automation (PSA) software designed to help project-based services organizations. The software helps in eliminating error-prone manual processes and replacing homegrown systems, thereby helping clients track time and expenses, invoice clients, schedule resources and manage projects.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>BigTime Software is a developer of online time and billing software for professional service firms designed to increase the profit margins of the business. The company offers practice management tools that help in maintaining invoicing in an accurate, timely and well-documented way, enabling clients specifically in accounting, architecture and engineering, consulting, creative, government contracting, IT services and law firms to run professional teams more efficiently, unlock potential revenue and improve profitability within the knowledge economy.</p><p></p>
