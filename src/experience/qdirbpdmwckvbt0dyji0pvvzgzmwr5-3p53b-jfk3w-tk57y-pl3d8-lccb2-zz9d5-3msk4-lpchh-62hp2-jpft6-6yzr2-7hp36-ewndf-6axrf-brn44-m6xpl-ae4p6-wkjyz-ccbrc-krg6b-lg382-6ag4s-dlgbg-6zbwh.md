  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>The company was acquired by RevOptix for an undisclosed amount on October 10, 2014.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>RevolutionEHR is a provider of electronic health record software for optometrists. They offer an online platform that allows patients to view their personal health records.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>RevOptix provides financial investment services. The company specializes in investments in the eye care industry.</p>
