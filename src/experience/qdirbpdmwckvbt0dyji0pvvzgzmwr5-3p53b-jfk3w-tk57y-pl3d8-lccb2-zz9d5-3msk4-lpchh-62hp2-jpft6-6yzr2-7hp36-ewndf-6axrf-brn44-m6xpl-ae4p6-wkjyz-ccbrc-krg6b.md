  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Latista was acquired by Textura Corporation (NYSE: TXTR) for $35 million on December 2, 2013.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Latista is a provider of mobile-enabled technology for construction project collaboration. The company provides mobile software designed for collaboration between the field and office for construction firms. Its software enables users to collaborate on mission-critical construction workflows, including quality management, punch lists, safety, electronic commissioning, and document management, including managing project specific plans and drawings.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Textura is a provider of on-demand business collaboration software to the commercial construction industry. The company's applications include construction payment management, Textura management dashboard, pre−qualification management, submittal exchange, and gradebeam.com.</p>
