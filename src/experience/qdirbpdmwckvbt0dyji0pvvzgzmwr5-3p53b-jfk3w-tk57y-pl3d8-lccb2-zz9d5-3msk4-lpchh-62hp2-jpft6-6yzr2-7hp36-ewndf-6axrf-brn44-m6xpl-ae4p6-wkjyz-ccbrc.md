  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Logi Analytics raised $27.5 million of Series C venture funding in a deal led by LLR Partners on September 27, 2013, putting the pre-money valuation at $121.75 million. Updata Partners and other undisclosed investors also participated in the round.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Company Details</h4><p>Logi Analytics is a provider of embedded business intelligence and analytics software. The company enables organizations to put real-time information to work by allowing users to create web-based business intelligence and analytic applications that can be integrated directly within organizations' applications, systems, and processes.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Investor Details</h4><p>LLR Partners is a private equity firm based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The firm seeks to invest in companies operating in the business services, education, fintech, healthcare, industrial, security, HR technology, and software sectors.</p>
