  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Field ID was acquired by Master Lock on February 21, 2013 for an undisclosed amount.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Field ID provides a SaaS, cloud-based inspection, safety compliance, and maintenance software. The company's software combines web-based and mobile technologies with the power of electronic identification (RFID and barcodes) to improve the efficiency, reliability, and reporting of workplace safety management, enabling manufacturers, distributors, and third party interceptors to appropriately lock out machinery, inspect facilities and equipment, manage corrective and preventive actions, and track assets.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Master Lock provides a broad range of innovative security and safety solutions for consumer, commercial, and industrial end users through our iconic brands: Master Lock, American Lock, and Sentry Safe.</p>
