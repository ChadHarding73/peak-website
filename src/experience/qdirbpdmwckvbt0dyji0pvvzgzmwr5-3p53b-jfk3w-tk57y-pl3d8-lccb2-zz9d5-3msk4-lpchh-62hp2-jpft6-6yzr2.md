  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Snagajob raised $27 million of Series C venture funding on March 2, 2011, putting the company's pre-money valuation at $131.68 million.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Company Details</h4><p>Snagajob is operator of a job search portal, an end-to-end platform of talent and workforce management that offers a fast and easy way to search for jobs. They also provide recruiting services, training, scheduling, and performance management to employers, enabling them to hire right fit workers.</p>
