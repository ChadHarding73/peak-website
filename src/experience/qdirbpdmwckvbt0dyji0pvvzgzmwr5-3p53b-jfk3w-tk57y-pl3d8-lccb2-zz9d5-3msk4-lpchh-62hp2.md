  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>AppCentral was acquired by Good Technology, a venture-backed company, on October 1, 2012, for an undisclosed amount.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>AppCentral is a provider of mobile application management software.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Good Technology is a provider of mobile messaging services for mobile operators. The company offers a platform for mobile service providers to deliver push email, calendar, contacts, and various corporate data to a set of devices. The company was acquired by BlackBerry Limited (TSE: BB) for $425 million on November 2, 2015.</p>
