<div class="row"><div class="col span-12"><hr class="rule"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>WorkWave was acquired by Chicago Growth Partners, Pondera Holdings and ParkerGale Capital through a $67.7 million LBO on August 30, 2012.</p><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><div class="row"><div class="col span-5"><h4>Seller Details</h4><p>WorkWave is a provider of cloud-based field service and fleet management solutions intended to help organizations with a mobile workforce transform their business. The company offers a range of services including marketing automation, lead generation, GPS tracking and monitoring, lead acquisition, intelligent route planning, and disaster recovery for field service and last-mile transportation companies.</p></div><div class="col span-1"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div><div class="col span-6"><h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Chicago Growth Partners was a private equity firm based in Chicago, Illinois.
