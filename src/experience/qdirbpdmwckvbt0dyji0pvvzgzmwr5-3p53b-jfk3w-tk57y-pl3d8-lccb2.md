  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>Odyssey Software was acquired by Symantec (NASDAQ: SYMC) on March 2, 2012, for an undisclosed amount.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>Odyssey Software, a provider of enterprise-class mobile and embedded device management software offers software products that enable developers to architect, build, deploy, and manage line of business applications for remote, wireless, and ruggedized handheld device deployments.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Symantec—now Broadcom—was a large security software company. The enterprise security business was spun out to Broadcom in 2019.<br /></p>
