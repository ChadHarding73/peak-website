<div class="row"><div class="col span-12"><hr class="rule"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>StarCite was acquired by Active Network (NYSE: ACTV) for $51 million on January 10, 2012.</p><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div><div class="row"><div class="col span-5"><h4>Seller Details</h4><p>StarCite, a provider of on-demand global meeting management services, provides a technology platform that simplifies meeting and event planning for corporations, hotels, venues, and meeting suppliers worldwide.</p></div><div class="col span-2"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div><div class="col span-5"><h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Active Network is a global marketplace for activities and events that connects participants and activity organizers while offering actionable business intelligence through data solutions and an insights platform. The company offers event organizers advanced SaaS technology that streamlines the administration of activities and events. It also offers marketing access through integrated marketing and consumer promotions that develop authentic relationships between brands and active consumers. The company was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Dallas, Texas.<br /></p></div></div></div></div>
