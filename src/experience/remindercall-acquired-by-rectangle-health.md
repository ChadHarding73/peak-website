  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>ReminderCall was acquired by Rectangle Health via its financial sponsor GI Partners for an undisclosed amount on November 1, 2022.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>ReminderCall is a Silicon Valley-based business that provides automated appointment reminder solutions for healthcare providers and other appointment-based industries. The Company offers an easy-to-use, comprehensive interface to customize, automate, and send millions of text, voice, and email reminders each month to minimize and streamline customer’s workflows while reducing appointment no-show rates.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>Rectangle Health, a leading healthcare technology company, empowers medical, dental and specialty practices with seamless and secure technology to drive revenue by increasing patient payments and streamlining practice management and payment processing. Since 1993, the company's innovative solutions have reduced administrative burden and rebalanced the ledger for its thousands of healthcare providers in the U.S., reliably processing billions of dollars in payments annually.</p><p></p>
