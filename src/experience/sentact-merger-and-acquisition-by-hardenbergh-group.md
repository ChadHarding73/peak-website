<p><strong>SAN FRANCISCO, CA – May 30, 2025</strong> – Peak Technology Partners is pleased to announce its role as the exclusive financial advisor to <strong>Sentact, LLC</strong> on its acquisition by <strong>Hardenbergh Group</strong>, backed by financial sponsor <strong>BV Investment Partners</strong>. In conjunction with the Sentact transaction, Hardenbergh Group acquired Preverity, a risk intelligence and predictive analytics solution. The three organizations have merged and now operate under the Sentact name, thus establishing a leader in patient safety, risk, and healthcare compliance solutions.</p><p><strong>A Unified Platform for Comprehensive Healthcare Solutions</strong> </p><p>The merger integrates Sentact's innovative digital rounding and compliance platform with Hardenbergh Group's expertise in physician credentialing and Preverity's predictive analytics capabilities. The combined platform delivers a seamless, data-driven experience that drives measurable results for healthcare organizations and executives leading finance, nursing, safety functions and medical staff services. </p><p><strong>Leadership Perspectives</strong> </p><p>Chris Dube, President of Sentact, expressed enthusiasm about the merger, stating, "<em>We are designing this platform for the future of healthcare leadership. Hospital executives don't just want reports – they want foresight. They want the ability to act before issues arise. That's what Sentact is building.”</em>  </p>
