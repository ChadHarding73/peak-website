  <h4>Deal Synopsis</h4><p>TeamUp was acquired by DaySmart Software, via its financial sponsors LLR Partners and Parthenon Capital, on September 1, 2022 for an undisclosed amount. The combined business will provide an operating platform to serve an integrated solution in the recreation and fitness industry in North America and Europe.  <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220908005168/en/DaySmart-Acquires-TeamUp">Press Release</a>.</p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Seller Details</h4><p>TeamUp is user-friendly management software for fitness businesses. Since 2012, TeamUp has developed a unique customer-centric software and service that has enabled thousands of business owners to build thriving businesses through strong connections with their customers.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4>Buyer Details</h4><p>DaySmart Software is committed to providing SMB and enterprise clients tailored business management solutions to help them run and grow their businesses. Since 1999, DaySmart has offered vertically focused cloud-based applications and payment processing solutions to businesses of all sizes, spanning across the recreation, vet, pet, salon, spa, and body art industries.</p><p></p>
