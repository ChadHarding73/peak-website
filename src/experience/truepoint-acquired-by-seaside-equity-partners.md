  <h4><strong>Deal Synopsis</strong></h4><p>GovPath (formerly TruePoint Solutions) was acquired for an undisclosed amount by Seaside Equity Partners on October 31, 2023.  <a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231031589871/en/Seaside-Equity-Partners-Announces-Partnership-with-TruePoint-Solutions-a-Leading-Provider-of-Government-Software-Implementation-Support-Services">Press Release. </a></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Seller Details</strong></h4><p>GovPath is dedicated to helping state and local governments lead the way in delivering better public service and building a future where both governments and the people they serve feel empowered, connected, and supported. The company provides software solutions for local government permitting and licensing, along with comprehensive support and managed services.</p><p></p>

  

 
  
  <h4><strong>Buyer Details</strong></h4><p>Seaside Equity Partners is a private equity firm based in San Diego. Seaside focuses on making control investments in leading providers of mission critical services in the Western U.S.</p><p></p>
