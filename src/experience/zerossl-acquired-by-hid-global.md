The acquisition aims to enhance HID's capabilities in providing secure communications for websites and strengthen its position as a leading provider of Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) solutions. With ZeroSSL's established expertise and automated e-commerce platform for SSL certificates, HID aims to expand its suite of offerings, reinforcing its commitment to safeguarding digital transactions. <a href="https://newsroom.hidglobal.com/hid-enhances-its-pki-offerings-acquisition-trusted-certificate-service-provider-zerossl">Press Release.</a> </p></div><div class="b b--spacer"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div><div class="row"><div class="col" style="--span: 5; --of: 12"><div class="b b--html"><h4><strong>Seller Details</strong></h4><p>Established in 2020, ZeroSSL is at the forefront of digital security solutions. The company simplifies the acquisition of SSL certificates through its automated digital e-commerce suite, serving as a global platform for website security. With a focus on accessibility, usability, and automation, ZeroSSL has gained the trust of over 2 Million customers worldwide, issuing nearly 1 Billion SSL certificates since inception. The company's robust offerings include an automated e-commerce platform for SSL certificates and integration services for lifecycle management.</p><p></p></div></div><div class="col" style="--span: 2; --of: 12"><div class="b b--spacer"><div class="spacer vsize-1"></div></div></div><div class="col" style="--span: 5; --of: 12"><div class="b b--html"><h4><strong>Buyer Details</strong></h4><p>HID powers the trusted identities of people, places, and things worldwide.  With offices all over the globe, HID's trusted identity solutions provide convenient access to physical and digital places.  Collaborating with governments, educational institutions, hospitals, financial institutions, and innovative companies, HID is dedicated to ensuring safe and efficient navigation in everyday life.  Headquartered in Austin, Texas, HID has over 4,500 employees worldwide and operates international offices that support more than 100 countries. HID is an ASSA ABLOY Group brand.</p><p></p></div></div></div></div></div>
