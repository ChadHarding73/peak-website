<p>2021 was a banner year for PEAK Technology Partners. The Company successfully closed thirteen software transactions. Those deals involved companies across multiple software industry verticals, including healthcare, fintech, HR, infrastructure, productivity, communications.  They included a variety of different buyers and investors, both strategic and financial sponsors, including BP Ventures, Thomas H. Lee Partners, Twitter, and Warburg Pincus, amongst others.  PEAK represented clients from multiple geographies including North America, South America, and Europe.</p>
