<p>2022 was a banner year for PEAK Technology Partners. The Company successfully closed eleven technology transactions. Those deals involved companies across multiple software industry verticals, including healthcare, real estate, fintech, HR, infrastructure, productivity, communications.  They included a variety of different buyers and investors, both strategic and financial sponsors, including LLR Partners, Parthenon Capital, Vista Equity Partners,  and Mainsail Partners, amongst others.  PEAK represented clients from multiple geographies including North America and Europe.</p>
