<p>2023 was a good year for PEAK Technology Partners in a challenging market environment. Matching the number of deals closed in 2022, PEAK successfully closed 11 transactions in 2023 across multiple verticals, including EdTech, GovTech, PropTech, FinTech, Cybersecurity, Data &amp; Analytics, Healthcare and Vertical SaaS.  Counterparties in those deals were diverse as well, including both strategic and financial sponsors such as Vista Equity Partners, H.I.G. Capital, Sallie Mae, The Riverside Company, and Seaside Equity Partners.</p>
