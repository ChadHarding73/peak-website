</span></p2></div></div></div><div class="col" style="--span: 7; --of: 12"><div class="b b--html"><p><br />CEO and founder Mark Wilson is a dynamic leader, successful founder, and advocate for building businesses that help people. With a goal of creating 10,000 jobs, he set up Chime’s contact centers in underserved communities across the U.S., creating  significant positive social impact and career opportunities. When his high-growth business needed capital to keep growing, Mark sought a minority partner to help him make an impact on more people. After casting a wide net, BBH Capital Partners rose to the top as the ideal partner for Chime.
