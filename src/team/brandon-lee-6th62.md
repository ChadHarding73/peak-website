  <p>Brandon is an Associate at Peak Technology Partners where he focuses on business development and the execution of M&amp;A and capital raising transactions for technology companies.</p><p>Prior to joining PEAK, Brandon worked as an analyst at Blueprint Equity, a technology-focused growth equity firm where he focused on deal sourcing and due diligence. Before Blueprint, he worked at CFG Wealth Management and Currnt, a B2B tech company, where he was primarily responsible for business development. </p><p>Brandon graduated Summa Cum Laude from San Diego State University with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a major in Finance. </p><p>During his free time, Brandon enjoys working out, golfing, hiking, and is a big martial arts fan.  </p>
