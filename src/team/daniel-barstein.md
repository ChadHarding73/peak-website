  <p>Daniel is a Vice President at Peak Technology Partners.  Daniel has over 10 years of experience as an investment banker, business development professional, and entrepreneur. Daniel has executed over $500 million of transactions over his career.</p><p>Prior to PEAK, Daniel was an Investment Banker at Arbor Advisors where he advised technology companies on M&amp;A and capital raising transactions. Prior to that, Daniel worked as an Analyst with Fidelity Capital Markets on the Sales and Trading team.  Daniel has also held several operational roles at startup technology companies.  Most recently, Daniel was an Account Executive at Funding Circle, a technology-focused peer-to-peer lending platform, where he led business development and partnership efforts while helping scale the business more than fourfold.</p><p>Daniel graduated from Dartmouth College with a B.S. in Psychology where he was a two-year letter winner on the varsity football team.</p><p>Daniel grew up in Birmingham, Alabama, cheers for both the Big Green and the Crimson Tide, and is an avid outdoorsman.</p><p><em>Daniel is a Registered Representative of Finalis Securities LLC Member </em><a href="https://www.finra.org"><em>FINRA</em></a><em> / </em><a href="https://www.sipc.org"><em>SIPC</em></a><em>.</em></p>
