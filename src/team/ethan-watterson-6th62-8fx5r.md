  <p>Ethan is an Analyst at Peak Technology Partners, focusing on business development, capital markets analysis, and M&amp;A and capital raise deal process execution.</p><p>Prior to joining PEAK full-time, Ethan was an intern where he assisted with business development, sourcing a deal that PEAK advised and closed the following year. Ethan was also a member of BYU’s Marriott School Strategic Management program and led student project teams consulting with Qualtrics and CHOICE Humanitarian. He also worked as part of a student team consulting with Entrata. </p><p>Ethan graduated from Brigham Young University with a Bachelor’s of Science in Psychology and a minor in Business Strategy. </p><p>Ethan enjoys rock climbing, spikeball, board games, and spending time with friends and family. </p>
