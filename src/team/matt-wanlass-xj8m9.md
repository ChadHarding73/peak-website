  <p>Matt is a Senior Associate at Peak Technology Partners and has a background in working with entrepreneurs. His goal is to find and empower technology company founders by executing M&amp;A and capital raising transactions on their behalf. </p><p>Prior to PEAK, he led deal sourcing and due diligence initiatives as an Associate at LFX Venture Partners, focusing on Series A - C supply chain technology investments. Additionally, Matt was an Associate at Peterson Partners, an investment firm with both venture and private equity strategies that comprise over $1B AUM. He created the firm's sourcing engine and spearheaded its Executive-in-Residence program, where he found and evaluated investment opportunities for operators sitting on Peterson's bench. Before that, Matt worked at a Peterson-backed AI technology startup called Airin.</p><p>Matt holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Finance from Brigham Young University. </p><p>Whether sprinting through the streets of SF or battling it out on a basketball, tennis, or pickleball court, you'll always find him on the move.</p>
