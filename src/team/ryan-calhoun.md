  <p>Ryan is a Director at Peak Technology Partners. Ryan has over 10 years of experience as an investment banker, operator, and investment professional, and has executed over $300 million of transactions over his career.</p><p>Prior to PEAK, Ryan was an investment banker at Arbor Advisors where he advised technology companies on M&amp;A and capital raising transactions. Ryan previously worked in Strategy &amp; Operations at Axial, a technology-focused platform for capital raising and M&amp;A transactions, where he was responsible for the firm's customer upsell and retention strategy. Ryan began his career as an Associate in the Asset Management Group at PNC Bank where he was responsible for client and portfolio management.</p><p>Ryan holds a B.A. from Franklin &amp; Marshall College, where he majored in Economics and minored in History. He was also a member of the varsity basketball team that attended the Division III Final Four in 2009.</p><p>In his spare time, Ryan enjoys snowboarding, golfing, music and is an avid sports fan.</p><p><em>Ryan is a Registered Representative of Finalis Securities LLC Member </em><a href="https://www.finra.org"><em>FINRA</em></a><em> / </em><a href="https://www.sipc.org"><em>SIPC</em></a><em>.</em></p>
