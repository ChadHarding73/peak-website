  <p>Taylor is a Vice President at Peak Technology Partners.  She has over a decade of software-focused investment banking experience. Taylor is also a full-stack software engineer.  She previously developed numerous proprietary software applications across the entertainment, fintech, and gaming verticals.</p><p>Prior to PEAK, Taylor worked as a Senior Associate at a boutique investment bank in Los Angeles where she focused on software M&amp;A and capital raise transactions.  Taylor also previously worked with the United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC) in Geneva, Switzerland, where she advised diplomats on key areas of child healthcare including nutrition and safety in conflict.</p><p>Taylor graduated magna cum laude from Pepperdine University where she triple majored in Economics, French, and International Studies.</p><p>Taylor grew up in Canada and is an avid traveler, baker, and music enthusiast.</p><p><em>Taylor is a Registered Representative of Finalis S</em><em>ecurities LLC Member </em><a href="https://www.finra.org/"><em>FINRA</em></a><em> / </em><a href="https://www.sipc.org/"><em>SIPC</em></a><em>.</em></p>
